How Termnest treats your email — and everything else

Termnest connects to your email and turns your school's messages into an organized, per-child feed of events, tasks, and fee reminders. That means an app is reading some of your email — which deserves a straight answer, not a policy link. Here it is.

Everything below is built into how Termnest works — a mechanism, not a promise. Where something is a promise, we say so.

The eight commitments

01We read only the school senders you approve

When you connect Gmail or Outlook, you approve your school's sender addresses. Termnest fetches full emails only from those senders — nothing enters your family's feed but the senders on your list. For everything else in your inbox, Termnest sees only that mail arrived — never the contents. Your bank statements, your work threads, your personal life: never fetched, so never stored, processed, or seen. Access to your mailbox is read-only — Termnest cannot send, delete, or modify anything.

02Email content is deleted after 30 days

The text of each school email is kept for 30 days — long enough for you to check any item against its source — then deleted. What remains afterwards is the organized items (the event, the deadline, the amount) and the short quoted lines from the email that prove them.

03Every date can be traced to the email that said it

Tap any event, task, or fee in Termnest and see exactly which email it came from, one tap away. No black box: if Termnest got something wrong, you can see why and fix it. And when Termnest isn't sure, it doesn't guess — uncertain items go to a "needs a look" tray for you to confirm.

04Ads can never use anything from your email — structurally

Today Termnest has no ads. When local partner offers arrive, they can be matched only on: your emirate, your child's age band, the school-calendar window, and interests you explicitly declare. Never anything derived from your email. This isn't a policy we follow; it's a wall we built — the ads system has no technical access path to mail data. Google's Limited Use policy independently forbids ad targeting from Gmail data, and we're glad it does.

05Your children never have accounts

There are no child accounts, no child logins, nothing for a child to sign into and nothing about your child that an advertiser can reach. You create a simple profile to describe each child (a name, a year group) so emails file to the right kid — you can edit or erase those profiles at any time. Termnest holds what the school already emailed you, organized. COUNSEL: confirm PDPL guardian-consent framing for child profile data.

06Delete your account and everything cascades

Deletion is self-service — a button, not a support ticket. When you delete your account: all your content is erased, Termnest's access to your email is revoked at Google's or Microsoft's side (not just ours), your calendar feeds go dead, and notifications stop. Backup copies are purged within 30 days — CONFIRM with Dev/counsel.

07No third-party ad or analytics SDKs in the app

None. No trackers from ad networks, no analytics companies watching how you use the app. The measurement we do is our own, on our own infrastructure.

08We log who accessed what — including us

Every access to your data is recorded in an audit log — and that includes access by the people who build Termnest. Termnest is currently built by a very small team with production access; our internal rule is that admin tooling shows aggregate numbers, not email contents, and any access to a specific family's data (for example, to fix a bug you reported) is logged and, wherever feasible, done with your knowledge. We'd rather tell you this plainly than pretend a small company has a big company's org chart.

Where your data lives

Your data is hosted in the European Union — Frankfurt, Germany: our database and authentication run on Supabase (region eu-central), and our servers run on Fly.io (region fra). We chose the EU for infrastructure reliability — Termnest's core promise is reminders that fire on time — and for a mature, well-understood data-protection environment. We say this specifically because vague answers about data location are how trust dies. COUNSEL: PDPL cross-border transfer analysis covers EU hosting plus the processing flows below.

How the AI processing works

To turn a school email into structured items, the text of that email is processed by Anthropic's Claude API (the AI service). Plainly:

  • Only email content from your approved school senders is ever sent for AI processing. Nothing else from your inbox — and never your passwords or email credentials, which Termnest itself never sees (sign-in happens on Google's or Microsoft's own pages).
  • Processing is transient: the text goes in, the structured items come out, and the AI service does not retain your content for its own use. Under Anthropic's API terms, content sent to the API is not used to train their models. COUNSEL/Dev: verify current Anthropic commercial terms and pursue zero-data-retention configuration before launch.
  • The AI's output is never blindly trusted: items are checked against the source email, and low-confidence results go to your "needs a look" tray instead of being presented as fact.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect a mailbox any time in Settings — Termnest's access is revoked at Google/Microsoft, and that mailbox's email content is deleted from our systems.
  • Erase a child profile any time — the profile and its items go.
  • Delete your account — everything cascades, per commitment 6. Self-service, immediate, no exit interview.

You can also revoke Termnest's access yourself, on the provider's side, at Google Account permissions or Microsoft account settings — it works, and we tell you so.
